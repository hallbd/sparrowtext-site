Privacy policy

This policy describes what SparrowText does today. If the app changes, this policy will change with it.

SparrowText is an app for planning and sending text messages to groups, operated by PocketMap Studios LLC (Utah). Website: https://sparrowtext.com. Effective date: November 27, 2026. Contact: support@sparrowtext.com.

What stays on your phone

Your contacts, groups, messages, attachments, saved instructions, message log, send history, reminders, and writing examples are stored locally. Send history records, for each send, who it went to, the date and what happened for each person (handed off to Messages or Mail, failed, skipped, or not sure). It does not keep the message text. It is left out of iCloud and computer backups, and you can clear it in Settings or with "Delete all data". A person you mark "never include" is remembered with that choice. When you use optional AI drafting, the data listed below is sent for that request. We have no account system or server copy of your local stores.

Contacts, messages and attachments are stored only on this phone and are left out of iCloud and computer backups. Export a CSV to keep your contacts. Messages and attachments can't be exported yet, so they won't move to a new phone.

Deleting the app or using "Delete all data" removes local data. CSV exports contain contacts, not messages or attachments. Previously created backups and exports cannot be revoked by the app.

Contacts

The app reads contacts only when you choose people in the iOS contact picker, or import a file you pick. It does not read your address book in the background.

Sending texts

The app never sends a message by itself. You review each message and confirm. Messages go out through your own phone and number using Messages (or the Shortcut you set up). We do not see them.

AI drafting (optional)

Off until you agree on a consent screen.

To write drafts, SparrowText sends the following through our server to Anthropic: the message description and instructions, standing instructions, approved past drafts for that message, voice samples, preferred sites, and placeholder names such as {FirstName}. When web search is switched on, Anthropic may search the web using the request.

Before sending, this phone replaces names matching saved contacts (including aliases), regardless of capitalization, and removes detected phone numbers and emails. Names of two or more characters are matched; one-letter names are not redacted. A name you type that is not saved as a contact, including an unsaved nickname or misspelling, can still be sent. Do not enter private details. Contact lists and groups are never sent.

The server adds fixed rules to every request so that the AI only drafts short text messages. It can decline other requests, and repeated declined requests can pause AI drafting for that anonymous ID for the month. When web search is on, the AI is told not to look up private individuals. Links in drafts are removed unless you typed them. The server keeps counts only, not message content.

You can preview the exact request before generating drafts. AI drafts and lookup notes can be wrong or inappropriate. Read and edit drafts and check lookup notes before using them. You can report either in the app. You can withdraw consent any time in Settings.

Our server passes the request on and returns the answer. It does not store or log message content. It keeps counts only (how many requests and searches, and token totals) against an anonymous random ID, to enforce usage limits and control cost. Our hosting provider also keeps standard network logs for each request to our server. These can include your IP address, the time, the type of app making the request and which part of the service was called, but not the text of your messages. We do not use them to identify you; the provider keeps them for about 7 days on our current plan (up to 30 days if the plan changes). The provider processes requests under its own terms: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/api-and-data-retention AI drafts can be wrong or inappropriate. A content check hides some drafts and lookup notes, and you can report either in the app. You always edit and confirm before anything is sent.

Reports to support

Tapping Report hides the AI output locally. Nothing has been sent yet. You can choose “Email this report to support” to review a message containing that output in your own Mail account and send it to support@sparrowtext.com. No report is uploaded automatically. If Mail is not set up, the app shows the support address so you can write from any email app. Reports you choose to email are received in our support mailbox and used to respond to and investigate the report. Do not include private contact details in reports.

Anonymous ID and notifications

The app creates a random ID stored in your phone's Keychain. It is not linked to your name, number or account, and is removed by "Delete all data". Reminders use local notifications on your phone; lock-screen text is generic unless you turn on details.

What we do not do

No ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data. We do not build profiles.

Children

For questions about children's privacy, contact PocketMap Studios LLC at support@sparrowtext.com.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the date. Questions or deletion requests: support@sparrowtext.com. Because we hold no content or account data about you, there is usually nothing on our side to delete; counts are tied to an anonymous ID only.
