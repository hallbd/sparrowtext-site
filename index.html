Group messages that still feel personal

SparrowText™ helps you write one message, then send it to each person as their own individual text or email, or as one group text, from your own number.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. Questions before then: support@sparrowtext.com.

What it does

  • Groups and messages. Pick people from your own contacts, save the messages you send often, and fill in each person's name automatically.
  • Separate texts or one group text. Separate texts are the default, so nobody sees who else received the message. If you would rather have one shared conversation, choose a group text. The app tells you what that means before you send.
  • You stay in control. You choose who gets each message. Nothing is sent without your review and confirmation, and nobody is selected for you.
  • Sent from you. Messages go out through your own phone and number (or your own email account). They are not sent from our servers.
  • Reminders. Set a reminder to send a recurring message, and review it before it goes.
  • Optional AI drafting. Ask for a few draft options in your own voice, then edit them. It is off until you agree on a consent screen.

Your contacts stay on your phone

Your contacts, groups and message history are stored on your device. We have no accounts and no copy of them. If you use AI drafting, the text of your request is sent through our server to Anthropic. Names matching your contacts and phone numbers or emails are removed on your phone first, but a name that is not saved as a contact can be missed, so you can preview the request before it goes. Details are in the privacy policy.
