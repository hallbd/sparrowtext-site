Common questions

Does SparrowText send messages by itself?

No. You review each message and confirm it. Messages go out from your own phone and number.

Can my groups be sent a message all at once?

By default each person gets their own individual message, and nobody sees who else received it. If you would rather have one shared conversation, choose a group text when you review the send; the app tells you what that means first. You choose who is included each time.

"Could not find the shortcut"

If you chose the Shortcut sending method, the Shortcuts app must contain a shortcut named exactly SparrowSend. Settings in the app explains how it is built. You can also use the Messages method, which needs no setup.

AI drafting does not work

Check that you have agreed to the AI consent screen, that you are online, and that you have not reached your monthly limit. A draft can be blocked if it appears to contain a contact's name; edit your instructions and try again.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

On your phone only. Settings > Delete all data removes it, or delete the app. See the privacy policy.

Moving to a new phone

Export your contacts as a CSV from the app and import it on the new phone. Messages and attachments cannot be exported yet.

Reporting a bad AI draft

Tap Report on the draft. You can then choose to email the report to us; nothing is sent until you do.
